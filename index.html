Our Story

More Than Paper.
A Brighter Tomorrow.

An examination answer book is more than paper. It is the recorded evidence of a student's knowledge, effort and aspiration. At veritaScript, we believe that its digital representation must preserve that truth faithfully — in every detail.

We design and build intelligent digitization solutions that help educational institutions, examination bodies and organizations capture, protect and manage handwritten knowledge with confidence.
